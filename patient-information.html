Before your visit

A little preparation can make the day feel clearer.

Use these general reminders as a starting point. Department-specific requirements and opening times can vary, so call the hospital to confirm details before you travel.

01

Confirm your appointment

Call the hospital to confirm the date, time, department, and any preparation instructions.

02

Bring helpful information

Consider bringing photo identification and a current list of medications or questions for your care team.

03

Plan your arrival

Ask about the verified address, parking, accessibility, and where to check in before setting out.

04

Ask for clarification

If an instruction is unclear, contact the hospital or your care team before your visit.
